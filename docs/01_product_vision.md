# Product Vision

## Project Name

### Business Pulse (Working Title)

## Purpose

Business Pulse is an offline-first business operating system designed for small and medium-sized businesses in Nigeria.

The goal is not to replace accounting software like QuickBooks. Instead, it focuses on helping business owners understand what is happening in their business in real time through simple data entry, dashboards, reports, and AI-powered assistance.

The system should be simple enough for a business owner with little technical knowledge to use every day.

## Problem Statement

Many small businesses record information using notebooks or Excel spreadsheets.

This creates several problems:

- It is difficult to know current inventory.
- Profit is not immediately visible.
- Customer debts are difficult to track.
- Reports take time to prepare.
- Information is scattered across different books.
- Business owners make decisions based on memory instead of data.

Business Pulse aims to solve these problems with an easy-to-use application that stores all operational data in one place.

## Target Users

Primary User

Small business owners who need an affordable operational management system.

Examples include:

- Grocery shops
- Provision stores
- Pharmacies
- Electronics stores
- Fashion stores
- Restaurants
- Schools
- Clinics

## Product Goals

The system should allow business owners to:

- Record daily business activities.
- Monitor inventory levels.
- Track customer credit.
- Track suppliers.
- Generate daily, weekly, and monthly reports.
- Search and filter business records quickly.
- Understand business performance through dashboards.
- Reduce manual calculations.
- Save time during daily operations.

## Core Principles

### 1. Offline First

The application must work without an internet connection.Internet should never be required for normal daily use.

### 2. AI is an Assistant

Artificial Intelligence is not the product.

AI exists to make the product easier to use.

Examples include:

- Voice-to-data entry.
- Natural language search.
- Automatic report summaries.
- Intelligent insights.

If AI is unavailable, the business management system must continue to function.

### 3. Simplicity

The application should require as little training as possible.

A business owner should be able to learn the basic workflow within minutes.

### 4. Reliability

Business data is valuable.

The application must prioritize:

- Correctness
- Stability
- Automatic backups
- Data integrity

over flashy features.

### 5. Reusable Product

Business Pulse is intended to become a reusable product.

The same core system should be installable for multiple businesses with minimal customization.

Only business-specific information should change, such as:

- Products
- Customers
- Suppliers
- Branding
- Business rules

The underlying software architecture should remain the same.

## Non-Goals

The first version will not attempt to become a complete accounting package.

Version 1 is not intended to replace enterprise software such as QuickBooks, SAP, or Oracle.

Instead, it focuses on operational visibility and day-to-day business management.

## Long-Term Vision

Business Pulse should evolve into a modular platform where additional capabilities can be added over time.

Possible future modules include:

- AI voice assistant
- Barcode scanning
- Invoice generation
- Receipt printing
- Multi-user support
- Mobile companion app
- Predictive inventory management
- Sales forecasting
- Customer analytics

## Definition of Success

The project will be considered successful when:

- A business owner can use it every day without assistance.
- Business information is easier to find than using notebooks or spreadsheets.
- Reports are generated automatically.
- The application reduces manual work.
- The software can be installed for another business with minimal changes.

## Guiding Question

Every new feature should answer one question before it is implemented:

> Does this help a business owner run their business more effectively?

If the answer is no, the feature should not be part of the core product.
